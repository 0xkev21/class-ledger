Does Class Ledger process KBZPay transfers?

No. Receive KBZPay transfers directly, then tap Paid.

Does the offline ledger need an account?

No. Events, fines, and rosters persist in AsyncStorage.

How does cloud sync protect ledger data?

Google, Email, or OTP uses Supabase auth.uid() RLS.

How does CSV ledger export stay safe?

Cells starting with =, +, -, @, tab, or CR get prefixed.

How do ledger themes and languages switch?

Toggle English, Burmese, dark mode, or 9 accents.
